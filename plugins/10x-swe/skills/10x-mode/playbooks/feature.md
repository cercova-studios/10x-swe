### Feature

1. Restate the behavior, who it is for, and the smallest change that delivers it.
2. When the design, interface, or scope is not already settled and approved, read and follow [[plugin/10x-swe/brainstorming/SKILL]] and stop at its approval gate.
3. Read and follow [[plugin/10x-swe/test-driven-development/SKILL]].
4. When the change moves module boundaries, read [[plugin/10x-swe/improve-codebase-architecture/SKILL]] for that seam. Do not turn the feature into a redesign.
5. If the work depends on a domain listed in 10x-mode, read that skill's `SKILL.md` before editing.
6. Verify the new behavior on the real trigger and label the evidence.
7. Stop. Switch to the finish playbook only when the user asked to integrate the branch.
