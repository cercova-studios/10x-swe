# Deduplication and knowledge maintenance

Use during skill creation/maintenance and when reviewing a skill's saved knowledge.
Scope the inventory explicitly; do not crawl unrelated vaults or session logs.

## Compare before changing

Read candidate bodies and, for skills, the full affected bundles. Search scores,
shared tags and titles identify candidates, not duplicates. Separate:

- Exact duplicates: identical content and provenance; use the existing reversible
  full-bundle procedure for skills. Published knowledge follows its own retention contract.
- Repeated claims or procedures: same meaning under the same conditions. Consolidate
  the explanation in one canonical location and link callers/readers to it.
- Complementary evidence: related theme, different mechanism, source, test or boundary.
  Keep distinct and link with a short reason.
- Revisions or contradictions: retain dates, versions, conditions and contrary evidence.
  Mark what extends, corrects or supersedes what; do not silently choose the latest.

A shorter synthesis must retain source identity, citation locators, claim versus
inference, limitations, negative results and applicability. Several notes from the
same session/source are not independent corroboration. Prune filler and duplicated
explanation, not safeguards, executable capability or inconvenient evidence.

## Skills

Keep one owner for shared instructions and add a conditional route from callers.
Do not collapse different triggers, permissions, tools or platform variants merely
because their wording overlaps. Preserve provenance/licenses and stable identities.
Follow maintenance.md for staging, incoming-link repair, backups, annotation,
validation and graph rebuild/check. Confirm representative requests still reach
all necessary capabilities after compression. Report what was removed and what
was deliberately retained.

## Saved insights and Obsidian

Respect the producing skill's storage contract. Source-insights entries are
immutable evidence records: do not rewrite or delete entries, analysis, manifests
or evidence to make a cleaner graph. Prefer a separate, clearly labeled curated
layer of topic syntheses and links to canonical records. Do not put curated text
in generated INDEX.md or assume reindex/search consumes it.

For an authorized Obsidian view, use stable, vault-relative Markdown links or
Wikilinks. Identically named note.md files need full entry paths, not [[note]].
One outgoing link already produces an Obsidian backlink; explicit reciprocal links
are useful only when each direction adds context. Label relations such as extends,
contradicts or applies, and distinguish editorial links from source claims. Use
focused topic hubs rather than connecting every pair with the same tag.

If the originals cannot be edited, keep relationships in curated notes or a separate
export. Label exports as derived snapshots, retain canonical entry IDs and evidence
links, and state how to refresh them. Do not imply an exporter or automatic sync
exists when it does not. Keep private evidence local unless export is authorized.

## Verify and stop

Check every changed link resolves within the intended vault/inventory, sources and
citations remain reachable, and compression preserves conditions and disagreements.
Report inventory scope, candidate groups, merge/link/keep decisions, actual checks,
and untouched evidence. Physical removal requires explicit authorization, inspected
exact targets, a backup/recovery plan and incoming-link repair. A cleanup proposal
is not deletion permission. Stop once the scoped maintenance is complete; do not
add a new indexing system merely to reduce repeated prose.
